COVER ART for issue No. 3 of a 1950s American funny-animal detective comic: "The Thing in the Keg Room". The logo band sits ABOVE this picture and a title ribbon and a small TV-monitor box will be laid over its BOTTOM fifth, so keep every face and the key action in the upper three quarters.
LANDSCAPE frame, the whole cellar at night, horror-movie poster staging played for laughs:
- Left, big and close: Officer Lyly at the foot of the wooden stairs, frozen in terror, eyes huge, tongue out, her yellow round sunglasses slid down to the very tip of her nose; she holds a FLASHLIGHT in her RIGHT paw only and its white beam cuts diagonally across the dark picture; a small steel beer keg is tumbling out of her left arm.
- Centre, where the beam lands: the round iron drain grate in its red lipstick circle, and through the bars the ONE huge acid yellow-green eye with a black slit pupil staring back up at her; teal tentacles with pink suckers curl up out of the grate into the frame.
- Back right, small, in a wash of red light: YOYO on her throne of red beer crates with the rabbit-ear guitar on her lap, smirking; the four hooded candle rats stand around the lipstick circle, their candles lit.
Colour: deep blacks and dark green-grey stone, the beam pale yellow-white, the eye and the grate glow acid green, the throne corner hot red; strong saturated four-colour printing.
