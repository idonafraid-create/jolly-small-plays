- Upper centre-left: the black iron fire escape caught in a white lightning flash; at its top the small black silhouette of a kitten with a big bow and an open umbrella.
- Right: the chain-link fence, and beyond it the bright headlight beam of a freight locomotive cutting through the rain.
- Foreground, in mid-air: a tiny gold bell falling, catching the light.
Colour: slate blue-grey night, neon pink, signal orange and the warm bulb, strong saturated four-colour printing.
